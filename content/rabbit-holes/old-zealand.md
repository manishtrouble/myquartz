# Where is Old Zealand?

One of those questions that makes you go "wait, actually..."

## The Answer

Zeeland is a province in the southwestern Netherlands. Dutch explorers named the islands they discovered after their homeland, hence "New Zealand."

## The Rabbit Hole Continues

But why did the Dutch explorers choose that specific province? And what about other "New" places?

- New York was named after the Duke of York
- New Hampshire after Hampshire, England
- New Orleans after Orléans, France
